Putting the Puzzle Pieces Together: Unifying a Fragmented Workflow for Consolidation of Duplicate Filings

Problem

There is nothing stopping an affirmative asylum applicant from applying more than once… and later consolidation of these duplicate applications is quite difficult. This manual process is highly tedious and complex—requiring detailed data validation across multiple applications and systems, while demanding system updates in a specific sequence to ensure consistency across all platforms.

Solution

We unified and streamlined the consolidation experience, introducing a centralized dashboard that brought all key actions and data into one place. This organized a previously fragmented process, reduced manual effort and cognitive load, and significantly improved efficiency for our users.

Impact

We recorded an 84% increase of admin closures right after release!
