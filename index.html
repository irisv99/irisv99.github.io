Hello again! A little about me…

Every project is an adventure — and as the designer leading the journey, my mission is to deeply understand the people, processes, and systems involved, then craft solutions that deliver measurable impact. As a full-stack designer, I use mixed-methods user research to design both user-facing experiences and behind-the-scenes workflows, moving from early concepts through lo- to hi-fidelity prototypes.

My work spans complex, high-stakes domains — from streamlining asylum case processing for the U.S. government, to transforming healthcare service delivery, and more.

I'm a UC Berkeley graduate with a BA in Cognitive Science, a minor in Data Science, and a Certificate in Entrepreneurship and Technology with additional graduate-level UX coursework through the School of Information. I currently live and adventure in New York City.
